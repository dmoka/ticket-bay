# Early-bird and discounts

## Early-bird

Book **at least 30 days before the event** and every ticket is **10% off**. The early-bird price ends exactly 30 days before the event starts. For refunds of early-bird tickets, see the refund policy.

## Group discounts

- 5–9 tickets in one order: 5% off every ticket.
- 10 or more tickets: 10% off every ticket.

## Discount codes

Enter a code at checkout. Codes are not case-sensitive. A code can expire, be used up, or be switched off; checkout tells you which.

## How discounts combine

Early-bird, group discount and a discount code **add up**, and the total discount is capped at 100%. The service fee is calculated on the price after discounts.
