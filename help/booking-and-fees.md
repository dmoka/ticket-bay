# Booking and fees

## Service fee

Every order has a **service fee of 3% of the ticket price after discounts, at least €1.00 and at most €20.00**. It is shown before you pay and it is not refundable.

## VAT

Prices include **27% VAT**. Your invoice shows the VAT part of the total.

## Your account

You need a TicketBay account to book. Your orders are under **My orders**.
