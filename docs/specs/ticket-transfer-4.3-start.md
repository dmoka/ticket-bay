# Ticket transfer

A customer can give the tickets of a paid order to a friend.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Only the order's owner can transfer its tickets.
2. The friend must have a TicketBay account; the transfer names them by email.
3. A transfer is possible only before the event starts.
4. After the transfer, the friend sees the ticket in My orders, and the owner no longer does.
5. A transferred ticket can't be refunded by the old owner.
